### Game Concept: The Chinchilla's Escape

The story takes place in a laboratory in South America, in a dystopian future. Chagas disease has mutated significantly, and people have started developing abnormal behaviors and physical characteristics that transform them. Their hearts begin to grow enormously as their bodies adapt to the disease, and humans become almost unrecognizable.

Some scientists in a laboratory begin experimenting on animals in an attempt to find a cure. However, the disease spreads to everyone, and eventually, all the scientists die. Humans no longer exist in this world.

Our chinchilla was born into a family of animals bred for experimentation. When the last scientist dies, the chinchilla is left alone in the laboratory. Now, she must find a way to escape.

Some animals have mutated because of the disease, while the infected humans have become unrecognizable.

The chinchilla must escape and help the other animals leave the laboratory so they can eventually help nature recover.

As she explores the laboratory, she discovers what happened to humanity and befriends other animals who help her escape.

In a dystopian future, a new strain of Trypanosoma cruzi, the parasite responsible for Chagas disease, begins to behave in unprecedented ways. The infection no longer affects only the heart and nervous system. In this fictional scenario, it triggers radical changes in the host's biology.

Human hearts begin to enlarge, their bodies change, and their behavior becomes increasingly erratic. Some infected people lose their ability to communicate. Others become violent, disoriented, or strangely unresponsive to their surroundings.

Governments collapse. Medical systems fail. Eventually, humanity disappears.

In an isolated research facility somewhere in South America, a colony of animals remains trapped. They were bred and modified in an attempt to understand the disease and develop a cure.

One of these animals is a chinchilla.

She has never seen the sky. She has never touched soil or felt rain. The world she knows consists of observation chambers, metal cages, flickering lights, and the distant sounds of machinery.

Act I — The captive

She is curious, cautious, and accustomed to the laboratory's routines. She knows which machines make noise, where food appears, and which lights mean danger. She has never needed to understand the outside world because she has never known it exists.

When the laboratory begins to shut down, she must learn to navigate an environment that was never designed for her survival.
